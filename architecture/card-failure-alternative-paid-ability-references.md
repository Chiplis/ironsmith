## Typed ownership

The existing finalizer already correlates a paid label with the unique verified alternative method on the same card. It now visits permanent trigger/activation headers and bodies as well as top-level self-replacement branches. Each ability has its own antecedent scope; one conditional arm cannot lend a payment antecedent to its sibling. It preserves the distinct default/replacement programs instead of suppressing unsupported markers without an executable method.

The complete paid-cost-tail grammar recognizes affirmative/negative payment with an optional exact `this turn`. `OptionalCostRef.payment_window` retains this constraint through typed lowering, final linking, artifact serialization and rendering. It is independent of the method's display label and mana representation.

The successful native cast finalizer writes `OptionalCostsPaid.cast_payment_turn` after staged costs are paid. Existing Foretell provenance and `reset_costs` ownership remain intact. Unrestricted queries continue to use the recorded casting choice. A current-turn query with an actual paid marker but no retained date is unknown. The engine reports the existing `ExecutionError::IncompleteEvidence`, which **is accepted by the actual incomplete-execution latch**. The previous unrecovered design's `UnresolvableValue` was not accepted by that latch and is deliberately not reused for this boundary.

The current shared checked external predicate owner carries supplied payment receipts through positive, negated and composite conditions. Native trigger admission, ordinary/immediate trigger resolution, prospective cost conditions and Value resolution use that ownership. Original-event capture and checked pending-trigger draining consume the error latch and roll back failed capture instead of silently dropping a trigger. Authored regression cases inspect the real latch and both native admission/resolution failures.

A dated empty receipt establishes the completed normal cast's known unpaid choice. Stack-to-battlefield preserves the receipt; other ordinary zone changes reset it. Spell copies inherit the original cast's payment choices and X without becoming another cast. Permanent copies start unpaid. Snapshot serialization retains the original date. Trigger admission and Value lookup can use only an exact departed-source snapshot, never a newer incarnation sharing a stable card identity.

## Method and body trace

- **Earwig Squad:** Prowl's declared alternative cost remains {2}{B}; current spell creature types are compared with positive combat damage to a player using the source controller/types at damage time. Goblin and Rogue each qualify. Its paid ETB targets an opponent, searches and exiles three cards, then shuffles that same opponent's library. The normal {3}{B}{B} branch does none of those actions.
- **Latchkey Faerie:** Faerie or Rogue combat damage enables Prowl {2}{U}. Flying remains independent. Its paid ETB draws one card, while the normal {3}{U} cast does not.
- **Leonardo, Leader in Blue:** Sneak {3}{W}{W} uses the real return-an-unblocked-attacker cost during a completed declare-blockers step. The paid permanent enters tapped, attacking the exact stored player/planeswalker if still a valid destination. Paid entry gives friendly creatures +2/+0 until end of turn; the separate {1}{W} activation grants first strike until end of turn.
- **Turncoat Kunoichi:** Sneak {2}{W}{B} has the same native return/window/destination owners. One selected opposing creature feeds the exclusive default temporary exile or paid permanent-exile branch. Source departure before default resolution prevents the temporary exile; departure after default resolution returns its exact exiled target. The paid branch remains exiled.
- **Karai, Future of the Foot:** Sneak {2}{W}{B} supplies the cast receipt. Actual combat damage to a player triggers a targeted return from the source controller's graveyard. Normal or earlier-turn Sneak returns the creature to hand. Sneak paid in the actual current turn instead returns it to the battlefield, including when the exact source has departed before admission.
- **Monastery Raid:** Freerunning {X}{R} requires Assassin-or-commander combat damage to a player under the caster's control at damage time. Real payment captures X. The exclusive alternative branch exiles X instead of two, and the common permission follows that branch's exact exiled collection through the end of the caster's next turn without adding flash.
- **Sandman's Quicksand:** Mayhem {3}{B} is a graveyard-origin alternative method with normal sorcery timing and the exact discarded card's current-turn history. The current history owner correlates the old discarded hand object to its immediate zone-change result rather than following stable identity through later incarnations. Normal {1}{B}{B} affects all creatures; paid Mayhem affects only opponents' creatures, each once, until end of turn.

Prowl/Freerunning's common history predicate now returns a checked result. It requires the exact event source's captured characteristics, rather than accepting a target/unrelated snapshot. A known matching event establishes the existential permission; missing source LKI is incomplete when no known match exists. Complete empty native history remains a known negative. This scope does not claim recovery of arbitrary missing turn histories.
