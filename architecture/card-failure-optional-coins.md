## Remaining Game of Chaos boundary

Game of Chaos is not counted as proposed complete. Its frozen body and failure
metadata remain intact in the fixture. A later bounded implementation needs:

- One declared opponent target, preserved through every round and every choice.
- The caster as the actual flipper/caller on every round. When the caster loses,
  the opponent's authority to continue is distinct from a coin-win event.
- A repeat-local value initialized to one and doubled after every accepted
  continuation, irrespective of whose branch ran. It must not overwrite X,
  a chosen number, an event amount, or an earlier random instruction receipt.
- Completed gain/loss instructions before the appropriate player decides:
  the caster after a win, the saved opponent after a loss. The continuation
  requires its own exact accepted/declined receipt; unrelated optional effects
  must not supply it through aggregate status or numeric counts.
- Checked multiplication and life-state arithmetic with whole-process rollback
  for pending decisions, invalid evidence, resource limits and native failures.
  A representational limit cannot become normal stopping or a reward.
- Native modifier/foreign-chooser, alternating result, independent-X, target
  invalidation, partial-life-change pending, overflow and clean-retry scenarios,
  in addition to both exact full-body compiler/artifact paths.
