## Honest cache and provenance limits

The changed state lives in native CombatState, not a compiled card definition.
A retained v14 card artifact can be consumed by the corrected runtime without
changing its payload. That compatibility does not authenticate the runtime or
make old exact images acceptable. Descriptive compiler_version and unsigned
runtimeVersion remain insufficient proof of compiler or engine identity.
Artifact format/schema/checksum do not detect a malicious consistently relabeled
payload. Genuine recompilation from original source and metadata, genuine v14
fixture/catalog generation, and matched current compiler/engine/verifier/glue/
WASM generation remain deployment prerequisites, not accomplished work.

The existing missing-original-v5 provisioning gate and missing genuine v14 golden
are unchanged. No historical artifacts, signed transcripts, published descriptor
bytes or hash domains are regenerated, renamed or relabeled here. No corpus
recovery totals or card-completion credit follow from these authored gates.


## Final native owner and lifetime

`CombatState.last_attack_declaration_step_players` is
`Option<BTreeSet<PlayerId>>`. Its typed public projection is
`lastAttackDeclarationStepPlayers: null | sorted u8[]` at the active checkpoint
root and in each Grand Melee combat lane. Null means absent/uncommitted evidence;
an empty list is a committed declaration with no direct player attacks.
The evidence remains with its combat after step exit, and is cleared on each
entry to DeclareAttackers in both schedulers. The fog reader independently
requires the current Combat/DeclareAttackers window. No projection claims it is
always evidence about whichever step is currently active. Team members union
only committed direct-player declarations. Attacks at permanents, attacking
nothing, and entering attacking do not invent direct-player declaration evidence.
Phase-wide history used by melee remains independent and unchanged.

The native author supplied `prevention_step_savepoint_tests.rs` covering real
RuntimeSavepoint capture/clone/exchange/restore, pending decision roots and
ReplayCheckpoint ownership. The active/lane projection gate is extended here to
require digest10 and distinguish explicit null, empty and sorted nonempty values
in both owners. Native reset/reentry, clone/branch, full-body legality and damage
scenarios are in `prevention_evidence_bodies.rs`; all remain UNRUN.
