# Historical block roles in complete destroy filters

Heat Stroke and You Cannot Pass! are two frozen baseline failures. The destroy
shape gate previously rejected their complete block-history clauses before the
shared filter reader could consume them. The gate now passes the entire proved
suffix to that reader. Unknown trailing conditions remain rejected.

The shared reader represents active blocking and passive becoming-blocked as
an OR, retaining the outer creature/controller requirements and any earlier
union conjunctively. It keeps a legendary or other partner description nested
in the existing historical-partner field. Current blocking, current legendary
status and damage history cannot substitute for those facts.

You Cannot Pass! uses the partner's characteristics at the declaration. The
official [Lord of the Rings release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/the-lord-of-the-rings-tales-of-middle-earth-release-notes)
confirm that the partner may since have ceased to be legendary or left the
battlefield. A missing or mismatched partner snapshot now records typed
incomplete evidence on the existing checked-action owner, rather than reading
the partner's changed current object. Current turn history uses exact ObjectIds;
blinked objects do not inherit combat history. Heat Stroke also includes an
attacker made blocked by an effect without inventing a blocking creature.

Full frozen direct/artifact scenarios are authored for real paid casts, actual
attack/block declarations, the real end-combat trigger, both event roles,
effect-driven blocked state, historical legendary changes/departure, unrelated
creatures, all-illegal resolution after blink, new-turn reset, native cloning,
checked missing evidence and strict unsupported tails. All are unrun. No build,
compiler probe, test, formatter or corpus execution occurred. No new wire field,
artifact version or audit action is introduced. Coverage awaits independent
source review and final integration; these are not verified recoveries.
