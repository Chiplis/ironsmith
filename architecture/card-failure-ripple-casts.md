# Ripple cast-resolution source proposal

UNVALIDATED. No builds, compiler probes, tests or corpus execution.
Five exact frozen Surging cards are pinned and source-reviewed through343b4d052.
They are proposed/unvalidated; no executed recovery is claimed.

Ripple is a typed numeric keyword, expanded into a Stack-only exact-source cast
trigger. Each occurrence remains independent. The new typed native RippleEffect
owns one optional reveal, ordered optional real casts, and the exact uncast
remainder on the library bottom in its controller's chosen order. It is not a
copy-spell shortcut. Each completed cast publishes/captures its own receipt before
another cast's costs can remove observers; recursive Ripple triggers wait for the
outer resolution to finish. Revealed and cast sets use exact ObjectIds.

The instruction owns pending/error rollback, including library order, public
history, deferred triggers, tags and payment mutations. Actual execution failures
propagate, distinct from ordinary cancelled cast proposals. Checked continuous
snapshots supply source names and face options. A countered source uses retained
exact departure/trigger LKI. Missing evidence errors rather than choosing a name.

The name qualification is on the revealed card, before choosing its cast face.
A split card matching one half can therefore cast either legal half. The native
cast owner still rejects aftermath from the wrong zone, other prohibitions,
illegal targets and unpaid additional costs. The free price cannot combine with
another alternative cost; ordinary X-zero and optional/additional costs retain
the existing cast transaction. Land plays are never offered.

Rules: pinned CR702.60 and the Thrumming Stone ruling in the official
[Double Masters 2022 release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/double-masters-2022-release-notes-2022-06-24).

Authored direct/artifact scenarios cover all five complete spell/permanent/Aura
bodies, paid original casting, recursive free casts, declined reveal, chosen
subset, short libraries, pending/malformed choice rollback, countered original,
multiple keyword instances and split-card pre-cast name qualification. Fresh
native encoding/materialization covers the direct schema, not only retained JSON.
All scenarios remain unrun; no measured card recovery is asserted.
