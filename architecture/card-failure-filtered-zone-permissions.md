# Filtered library and graveyard permissions

UNVALIDATED source foundation. No compilation, build, tests, formatting or corpus
replay ran. This checkpoint adds **zero whole-card proposals** while the explicit
private-visibility and face-down prerequisites below remain open. The exact

## Typed permission root

A complete named grammar consumes the filter, mixed land/spell alternatives,
origin, usage limit and an optional exact permission-local flash rider. The land
and spell arms share one typed grant. Land-only and cast-only domains remain
distinct, and an implicit battlefield/stack noun is not mistaken for an origin.
Unknown trailing riders and foreign-owner forms are declined. Rendering retains
the complete surface of that strictly recognized production; behavior uses the
typed fields and never dispatches on names or display strings.

`GrantSpec.top_card_only` and its native `PlayFromConstraints` counterpart check
the actual current library top, including direct action validation. These new
scope fields must be present in a decoded artifact/carrier: old data cannot
silently widen a top-only permission. Announcement captures the exact grant
identity, origin and timing before moving the card. A later top change does not
invalidate the already announced card or cause a newly exposed card to inherit
its receipt. Use limits are spent by the completed-cast owner and survive phasing;
a new source incarnation gets a new identity.

A plain linked-face cast now carries `SplitOtherHalfPlayFrom` with no alternative
cost rather than losing its granting source. Existing alternative indices remain
`Some(index)` with their existing JSON numeric representation. Face queries
install the chosen face under its actual ObjectId in an isolated continuous
snapshot, so characteristic lookup cannot accidentally return the front face.
All affected spell enumerators propagate discovery errors. Land enumerators no
longer discard qualifying MDFC backs using a front-face filter. Normal granted
permanent casts enter the battlefield; Adventure and genuine exile-requiring
alternative costs keep their separate destination rules.

## Sourced face-down follow-up (unvalidated)

`FaceDownPlayFrom` is appended to the native/wire casting routes. Morph,
megamorph and disguise use their public face-down characteristics when choosing
an exact zone grant, cost three before applicable adjustments, retain the
source/origin receipt and share its completed-use budget. A permission does not
invent a face-down keyword or ignore timing. Chosen-face discovery remains
checked; ordinary alternative costs are not combined with the face-down cost.

Peers receive only the public keyword claim and exact source/origin action.
Both command disclosure and placeholder replay recognize the new route without
publicly opening the physical card. The engine recomputes the source action to
reject forged grants, wrong origins, deeper cards and inactive hosts. The
existing identity-obligation ledger records the actual origin (including
library), and the later authenticated opening must prove the claimed keyword.
This reuses the existing proof/anchor owner, without accepting card identity
from the command. The two native direct/artifact and two production-helper Wasm
scenarios, plus UI identity/disclosure assertions, are authored and unrun.
The complete permission family still contributes zero new proposals pending
bounded review and the three remaining full-body implementations.

## Selected-use completion and Fourth Doctor (unvalidated)

program on its one filtered land/spell permission. Land play chooses among
available permissions before movement, after installing the chosen face;
