## Typed Class ownership

A Class is not a level-counter ability. The existing Class materializers wrap
later statics in source-only conditional grants at the relevant Class designation.
That reader therefore acquires an Effect origin, while its base exile trigger
belongs to the enclosing rules text. Erasing all Effect or Level origins would
cross-link independently acquired rules text.

The front end now carries `ActivatedAbilityKeyword::ClassLevel(u32)`, appended to
the existing keyword enum. The exact Class binder uses this semantic fact rather
than labels or the legacy restriction string. It inventories all five complete
ability scopes: the base face-down top-library producer and exact durable viewer,
level-two and level-three mana-only sorcery activations, the creature-you-control
menace grant, and the final complete linked play permission with its AnyColor
cast rider. Any extra scope, producer, private-pool qualification, altered tier,
activation effect, or unsupported cost prevents pairing. The immutable pair is
hashed from the complete typed definition and stamped on the producer and reader.

`GrantSpec.linked_exile_class_level` marks only the proved final reader. Its
runtime origin captures an exact pair/level bridge only when generated by the
matching static source-only Class gate. That bridge retains its generating host
and parent acquisition. Pair capture projects this one wrapper to the parent;
ordinary Effect, Temporary, Counter, Level and Borrowed origins retain their
existing distinctions. Borrowed readers retain their outer acquisition and exact
donor incarnation. Native readers carrying only the metadata cannot bypass the
actual gate: collection requires the captured matching Class bridge.

Two bounded late-discovery corrections support a complete acquired body. A
SetAbilities group containing the proved Class-linked wrapper can emit its later
statics. Its source-relative Class gates use the recipient's Class designation,
including when the acquiring effect belongs to another object. This exception
requires the exact acquiring effect occurrence and the marked complete group;
unmarked legacy source-relative grants keep their existing route. The linked
reader's own gate is likewise kept on its receiver. No live donor lookup changes
an already captured identity.

## Complete behavior

Combat damage from a creature the Class controller controls exiles the damaged
player's top library card face down. The resolving trigger's controller receives
persistent private inspection for that exact exile incarnation. This knowledge
is distinct from the live level-three play authority and from linked membership.
Level two and three retain their printed colored mana costs, previous-level
requirements and sorcery timing. Level two grants menace to the controller's
creatures. Level three supplies an exact selected permission with AnyColor only
while casting through that grant. Normal spell/land timing, land limits,
additional costs and colorless requirements continue through the previously
reviewed exact-selection payment owner. Source-only PlayFrom cannot claim it.

Control change updates live play authority while prior inspection knowledge
persists. Ability loss and phasing suspend the static reader; regaining the
ability or phasing in can resume it for the same Class incarnation. Card or Class
leave-and-return never adopts an earlier incarnation's membership or entitlement.
Pending trigger copies retain the original owner and viewer even if the source
leaves. A whole rules-text acquisition and a second acquisition of the same typed
body remain distinct, including a foreign grantor's SetAbilities group.

CR 406.3 additionally ends inspection entitlement when cards become part of a
shuffled face-down pile. The engine has no such exile-pile shuffle owner in this
source boundary. That interaction remains explicitly held, as in the earlier
private-inspection packets; this packet does not implement or count it.

## Remaining blind-play owner

A new controller can have the unqualified all-card play permission without the
old trigger controller's private inspection entitlement. This is distinct from
both persistent knowledge and current static authority. [CR 406.3a, 406.3b and
601.3f](https://media.wizards.com/2026/downloads/MagicCompRules%2020260619.pdf)
permit the unqualified case and place the face-up transition immediately before
announcement; the look requirement for qualified spell permissions does not turn
this all-card permission into private inspection.

The existing action proposal owner (`decision/legal_actions.rs`) consults the
hidden physical face's land/spell type, mana affordability and alternative methods
before announcement. The WASM adapter (`wasm_game_impl/helpers.rs`,
`build_action_view` and `redacted_action_label`) hides the name but distinguishes
"Cast hidden spell" from "Play hidden land" and retains the detailed action ref.
That can reveal hidden characteristics to a new controller before a blind play is
announced. This packet does not implement a blind face-up announcement transaction
or hide those pre-announcement distinctions. Its controller-change scenarios
establish authority and knowledge only. Full-card credit is held until this owner
is closed; omission of fields or ordinary visible-card cast tests cannot prove it.

The [official release notes](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/adventures-forgotten-realms-release-notes-2021-07-09)
also confirm separate tracking for each Rogue Class. The exact host/acquisition
membership introduced here retains that distinction.
