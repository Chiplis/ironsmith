## Compiled model and meaning

Three new structural fields cross the compiled-model boundary:

- `PreventDamageEffect<E>.damage_filter: DamageFilter`
- `PreventAllDamageToTargetEffect<E>.damage_filter: DamageFilter`
- `PreventAllDamageToTargetEffect<E>.source_color_of_your_choice: bool`

Missing filters structurally default to all damage; the missing color-choice
flag defaults to false. Current serialization emits the explicit defaults.
The previous unlimited `combat_only` field remains and is conjoined by the
interpreter with the new filter. Native encoding retains the complete filter,
original target, duration, shared-recipient/source-choice flags and child
program. Defaulting cannot restore a restriction that the old compiler dropped,
and model reserialization does not preserve original signed bytes.

Copular source-only, unconditional nonempty AddSubtypes construction changes
serialized `Ability.functional_zones`. Printed/copied CDA origins and ordinary
temporary grants retain their different provenance and functional-zone owners.
Explicit source zones, conditions and chosen-type dependencies keep their bounded
scope. Loading an old definition deliberately retains its stored zone list;
loading must not rerun construction defaults. Regeneration is necessary even
though this part adds no enum or model field.

The fresh copular correction also gives explicit `chosen creature type` its
existing AddChosenCreatureType family for land and land/creature-union recipients.
It must not inherit recipient-based basic-land-choice inference. Bare `chosen
type` keeps the established land inference. The two typed payloads and choice
stores remain distinct, with ordinary battlefield functional zones. The boundary
adds an authored source/artifact/materialization scenario for both constructions;
it does not claim that executing or reparsing either has been validated.

Destination/source productions preserve source-zone qualifications, per-object
owner versus selected-player controller, and exact delayed death-arrival
incarnations. Unsupported source/destination residue fails closed. A same-name
or same-stable-ID object cannot substitute for the retained exact arrival.
These use existing model vocabulary but change compiled meaning and execution.

Prevention source properties use current characteristics while the exact source
exists, and exact departed-source LKI after departure. A stale snapshot cannot
override a live source's changed type or color. Blink does not transfer authority
to another incarnation; the narrowly authorized spell-to-permanent transition
remains explicit. Target/reference recipients bind at registration, while live
recipient filters remain live. This includes the recovered Focus/Chameleon
corrections; new fields alone would not establish the required semantics.

## Added source-counter and static-prevention semantics

The canonical `RemoveAnyCountersFromSourceEffect` keeps its existing stable kind
and complete ordered payload: `counter_type`, `display_x`, `remove_all`.
The published core already used that named JSON shape; the bridge now transports
that same core type through normalization, interpreter, decoder and native
encoder. Null/built-in/named counter types and both boolean values, including
`remove_all` taking precedence over `display_x`, retain their existing encoding.
No new serialized field, default, enum variant or reordered field is introduced.
The native captured-payment projection flag is deliberately absent from artifacts
and public programs. See `card-failure-source-counter-payload-bridge.md`.

Prepared source-counter payments preserve the requesting effect's cause through
original and completion phases, including source, controller and resolving spell;
ordinary costs retain the existing source/payer fallback. Captured counter-quantity
owners report checked physical original removals separately from nominal X and
successful payment acknowledgement. Accepted zero and prevented payments remain
accepted; prevention/replacement facts and actual counts belong to their owned
child observations. Pending, declined, failed and impossible paths cannot acquire
acceptance. Direct and captured energy retain nominal parent count/requested
quantity/ChosenNumber. Direct crew and staged counter callers use nominal accepted
payment guards and keep their legacy aggregate result. These are native execution
owners, not newly serialized receipt fields or public recovery state.

The eleven fixed static reductions retain the specialist
`PreventDamageToYouFromSourceFilter` payload and true prevention action. The
specialist/general grammar domains are disjoint. The five unquoted Aura `dealt by`
productions now construct Aura-owned `PreventMatchingDamage`, using canonical
`Enchanted`/`IsTaggedObject` source selection, all-recipient filters, and their
original combat qualification. The static ability stays battlefield-scoped;
its source/controller and lifetime belong to the Aura. Genuine quoted grants
retain recipient ownership. No decoder rewrites an old grant into the new rule.

The live attachment owner must exist on the battlefield and be unphased. Its
current attachment must match the damage source's exact incarnation before
reading that source's characteristics or departed-source LKI. Moved/detached,
phased, absent or blinked owners/hosts cannot revive a stale attachment; matching
characteristics alone do not authorize another incarnation. This covers top-level
positive Enchanted/Equipped identity tags and inverse attachments requiring the
replacement source. It does not rewrite the broader filter grammar. Source,
controller, ability-loss and lifetime outcomes are intentional behavior changes.
See `card-failure-static-prevention-regressions.md` for actual-owner UNRUN cases.
