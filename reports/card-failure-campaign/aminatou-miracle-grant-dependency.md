Current-ability scanning does not duplicate a receipt-owned notice. A moved/new hand incarnation cannot inherit the draw receipt. The unit Miracle effect validates the exact draw/card/drawer/snapshot, obtains the casting actor from the resolving ability, and invokes the existing resolving-effect cast proposal/payment owner. Castable linked-face selection uses the same native projection for Room/split, Adventure and modal faces. Captured cost recipes survive source departure and keep colored, hybrid, Phyrexian and X symbols. Announced X and selected hybrid symbols consume the base's generic reduction before mandatory/additional/optional costs or taxes.
